# Commercial licensing

This project is **source-available, not open source**. Its code is public and free for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). Any commercial use needs a separate, paid commercial licence from the copyright holder.

This page explains the split in plain words. It is a summary, not the licence: where it and [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) disagree, the licence governs, and a commercial licence exists only as a written agreement signed by the copyright holder.

## What is free

The noncommercial licence covers, with no fee and without asking:

- Personal use: study, hobby projects, experiments, private entertainment, with no anticipated commercial application.
- Use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations and government institutions.
- Any other purpose that is not commercial.

Within those purposes you may run the software, change it, build new works on it and share copies, as long as every copy carries the licence terms and the `Required Notice:` line.

## What needs a commercial licence

Anything done for a commercial purpose, including:

- Selling the software, a changed version of it, or a product that contains it.
- Offering it, or something built on it, as a paid or revenue-generating service.
- Using it inside a business to produce work for pay: animations, sprites, tilesets or other assets for a client, a paid game, an advertisement or any other product sold or monetized.
- Using it as an internal tool in a for-profit company.

The last two matter for an art tool. The licence does not claim ownership of what you draw; it governs the act of running the software. Running it for a commercial purpose is outside the free licence, so the output of that work is covered by a commercial licence, not by the noncommercial one.

If you are unsure which side a use falls on, ask before you start.

## Commercial terms

A commercial licence is granted case by case. Its terms, set in a written agreement, cover:

- Which uses and products it applies to.
- The payment: a fee, a royalty on revenue from the licensed use, or both.
- Reporting and payment schedule when a royalty applies.
- Support, updates and term.

No commercial rights exist until that agreement is signed. Using the software commercially without one is a violation of the noncommercial licence.

## How to ask

Open an issue titled `Commercial licence` at <https://github.com/Coaxus-ux/animations-ngine/issues>, saying who you are, what you want to build and how it earns money. Do not put confidential details in the issue; the conversation moves to a private channel from there.

## Contributions

Selling commercial licences is only possible if the copyright holder has the right to license every line of the project. That is why every contributor signs the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) before a change is merged. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-and-the-cla).
